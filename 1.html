The process

Three frames to motion

No node graphs, no manual keyframing. Load a still, describe the move, render the take.

01

Load the still

Drop a photo or paste a URL. JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 20MB — anything with a clear subject works best.

Note

Center the subject and keep the light even. Backlit or cluttered frames animate less predictably.

02

Direct the motion

Describe the movement in plain language — wind in the hair, drifting smoke, traffic at dusk. The model reads intent, not coordinates.

Example prompt

“Gentle breeze through hair, subtle sway, cinematic feel, 24fps.”

03

Render the take

Watch the frame animate in seconds. Grade the color, set the length, export as MP4, GIF or WebM.

Advanced

Cinematic color grading and motion-speed controls are available before export, not after.
